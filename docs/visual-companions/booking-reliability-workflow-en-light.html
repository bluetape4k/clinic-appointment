Simulation · one decision

1

Trusted event

Receive NO_SHOW or CANCELLED with typed responsibility and source version. Names and phone numbers stay outside this boundary.

2

Effective policy

Pin the immutable tenant baseline plus clinic override. Defaults are a 180-day lookback, three-event thresholds, and 24-hour cooling-off.

3

Bounded history

Read at most 100 rows for the clinic/member scope and keep the highest source version for each event ID.

4

Pure evaluator

Count only patient-responsible events. Clinic, operational, and unresolved responsibility never becomes a patient restriction.

5

Immutable decision

Persist verdict, counts, reason codes, up to 32 trigger IDs, expiry, and a SHA-256 digest.

6

Booking gate

OFF preserves behavior, SHADOW observes, and ENFORCE gates new provisional/new-confirmed paths.

ELIGIBLE

Active thresholds are not reached or a staff override is valid. Continue as an automatic offer candidate.

REQUIRES_STAFF_APPROVAL

The clinic sends a threshold hit to staff review instead of an automatic same-day offer.

RESTRICTED

Exclude the automatic same-day offer. An existing CONFIRMED appointment remains unchanged.

STALE / UNAVAILABLE

Fail closed in ENFORCE and retain only the bounded correlation reference when a snapshot or store is not trustworthy.

Decision history · why this shape

Choice B

Reevaluate PROPOSED and HELD, while preserving existing CONFIRMED. A new direct confirmation checks the same gate immediately before commit.

Privacy boundary

Pass only opaque MemberId from the member database. Staff explanations use allowlisted reasons and bounded IDs.

Large-clinic protection

History/trigger caps, opaque cursors, DB leases, idempotency digests, backoff, and clinic allowlists control reevaluation bursts.

Rollback

V17 is additive. If readiness or canary evidence fails, empty the allowlist and return to SHADOW or OFF.
